# 0.34.0 — The battle overview stops drawing the dead

- **Squares no longer sit on your own people.** A killed monster keeps its place
  in the game's combat list and keeps its last square, even though the game's
  own Combat View stops drawing it. The overview was still marking it — so once
  your party advanced onto that square, it looked like an enemy marker sitting
  on one of your characters. Combatants at zero hit points are now left out.
- **The numeric keypad reaches the game.** The app's key table stopped short of
  the keypad entirely, so a hardware keypad did nothing at all; only the
  on-screen numpad worked. Combat movement needs the keypad for its diagonals —
  1 and 3 step up-left and up-right — and now a real keyboard can do it.

The sides were never wrong, by the way. Both the labelling at the start of a
battle and the pairing after a character moves were checked against the game's
own memory and are correct.

[Download the public APK](https://github.com/huntergdavis/poolrad-macmaps/releases/download/v0.34.0/poolrad-macmaps-0.34.0.apk)
and install over the existing app. **Bring your own ROM, Mac system and game**;
those are still not included.
