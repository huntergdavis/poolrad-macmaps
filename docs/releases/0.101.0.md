# 0.101.0 — Rest until healed in one step

F52 adds **PoolRad → Rest until healed**. It does what a full, uninterrupted
camp rest would leave behind, for the whole party, in one step: hit points to
maximum, Unconscious and Dying characters awake and Okay, and every spell
chosen at Memorize made ready. A confirmation lists exactly who will be
healed, who wakes, how many spells memorize and who is beyond rest before
anything is written, and a notice afterwards says what happened.

**The game clock is not advanced.** A real rest would move it by hours or
days; this one leaves it where it is, and says so here as the backlog asked.
Also untouched: the dead, petrified and absent (no rest fixes those), poison
and helplessness (no rest cures those), and your saves (nothing is saved).
The option is refused during a fight and whenever the party does not read
cleanly.

This is the project's third write into the running game, authorised in
DESIGN.md as restoring memorised spells and the party after rest. It walks
the roster with every check the party reader makes and changes only the bytes
named above.

Validation: 742 Java tests; the native party test proves the exact
bytes changed for a hurt caster, a Dying and an Unconscious member, none for
the dead or petrified, none on a repeat and none on any refusal, under the
sanitizers; universal Mac II build; live emulator acceptance in docs/PARTY.md.
