# 0.52.0 — Tap the header to find yourself

On a full sixteen-by-sixteen map with walls, doors, footprints and notes on it,
the one small arrow that is you is genuinely hard to pick out.

**Tap the header and your square wears two rings for three seconds.**

- **Rings, not a fill** — the square underneath is the thing being pointed at,
  and filling it would hide the arrow, the footprints and anything written
  there.
- **Two of them**, because one at this size reads as another map symbol.
- It is a way of looking, not a way of doing: nothing moves, nothing is written,
  the tap creates no note, and it fades on its own.

The check holds it to all four, including that the pane comes back
pixel-identical once the rings have gone.

**Verified:** 557 Java tests, six native probe suites, 56 render checks across
four suites, the APK check.

[Download the public APK](https://github.com/huntergdavis/poolrad-macmaps/releases/download/v0.52.0/poolrad-macmaps-0.52.0.apk)
and install over the existing app. **Bring your own ROM, Mac system and game**;
none are included.
