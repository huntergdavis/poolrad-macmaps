# 0.35.0 — A cross for whoever is down

- **Party members who are down are marked with a cross** on the battle overview,
  so you can see at a glance who has fallen and where they are lying — which is
  what you need before you can go and bandage them. The header counts them:
  "6 of yours · 6 others · 2 down".

This corrects 0.34.0, which stopped drawing everyone at zero hit points. That
fixed enemy markers lingering where an enemy no longer was, but it also hid your
own casualties, which is the opposite of helpful.

The difference now comes from the game's own record of each character's
condition rather than from their hit points: unconscious, dying, dead and
petrified are all *down but still on the square where they fell*. A monster in
that state is still not drawn, because the game's own Combat View stops drawing
it too.

[Download the public APK](https://github.com/huntergdavis/poolrad-macmaps/releases/download/v0.35.0/poolrad-macmaps-0.35.0.apk)
and install over the existing app. **Bring your own ROM, Mac system and game**;
those are still not included.
