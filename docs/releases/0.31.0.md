# 0.31.0 — It stops flickering

- **Nothing blinks away any more.** The companion reads the running game four
  times a second, and the game is not always in a readable state at that exact
  instant — least of all in a fight, where it is rewriting the characters
  continuously. Every one of those moments used to blank the party, the
  position or the battlefield for a fraction of a second. Each now holds its
  last good reading for five seconds before admitting it has lost it.
- The position does the same: if it cannot be read this instant, the last known
  position stays on the map.
- **A fog-of-war button** next to the footprints button, the same size. Four map
  squares, one open and three covered; crossed out when fog is off. Both
  buttons are the same settings as the Info checkboxes.
- **Bookmark, Check off and Link a map flag are gone** from the journal. Reading
  a proclamation is now reading a proclamation. Anything you had bookmarked or
  linked is still stored and still in your notebook backups.
- The exploration trail dialog lost its wall of caveats.

[Download the public APK](https://github.com/huntergdavis/poolrad-macmaps/releases/download/v0.31.0/poolrad-macmaps-0.31.0.apk)
and install over the existing app. **Bring your own ROM, Mac system and game**;
those are still not included.

Known: the battle overview may show the party/enemy markers on the wrong side of
the fight once it is under way. The squares are in the right places; which side
each one belongs to is being re-derived from a live battle rather than guessed.
